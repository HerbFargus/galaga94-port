Ok, here are the instructions for this fabolous game :

1) START NEW GAME / TITLE SCREEN
Press F2 at the title screen to change difficulty level.
Press F1 at the title screen to start a new game.
Press F10 at (guess where) to quit and exit to DOS.
Press ESC during the game to return to this screen.

2) ACTION KEYS
Use the following keys during the game to control your fighter:
Cursor left  : Move left
Cursor right : Move right
Cursor up    : Start (use it to continue certain occasions)
Right shift  : FIRE ! (use it to blast all that alien scum ...)
Enter        : MEGABOMB ! (limited...)
ESC          : Exit 

3) THE REAL SENSE OF THIS GAME
Did you ever wish to blast tons of little pixel images out of your
home computers universe ? OK. Here you go....
This is not really a sophisticated game but it should be a lot of fun
for those who like it.

4) WEAPONS
You start with a ship capable of firing 3 rockets a time. You can increase
this number by collecting extras. You also start with 3 mega bombs. Mega 
bombs destroy all falling bombs and enemies within a certain range.

5) EXTRAS
There are a six different kind of extras :
I  ) SIDEWINDER : Your ship can carry two sidewinders and every sidewinder
                can carry 3 rockets. As your ship is only able to hold
                6 rockets, this makes a full equiped ship with...
                ah yes : 12 rockets !
II ) ROCKETS    : Catching this extra gives you +3 rockets, if your ship is
                able to get them (sidewinders...)                 
III) MEGABOMB   : ..really a fine thing. A megabomb destroys all bombs and
                enemys within a certain distance.
IV ) SHIELD     : Makes you invulnerable for 10 seconds.
V  ) EXTRA LIVE : Extra life ...
VI ) SCORE      : Increases your score (1000 Points)

Everytime you have blasted away 20 enemy ships, a bonus bringer comes up,
it is a little green ship, and an acustic warning can be heared.
