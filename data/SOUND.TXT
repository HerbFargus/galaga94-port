This version of GALAGA 94 supports digital sound fx. The standard driver
for the Sound Blaster V2.0 (Creative Labs) is included. This driver is
compatible only to some cards. If you have a SB Pro or SB 16 / 32 or any
other Card with a higher standard than the SB 2.0, you have to do as fol-
lows to make it work with GALAGA:

Search for a driver which supports the CT-VOICE format. Any Creative Labs
soundcard has such a driver, only the names are slightly different. For
Example the driver included in the SB16-Package may be called CTV16.DRV or 
so... I am sorry that I did not have the time to collect all this drivers
and include them.
When you have found your driver, copy it into the GALAGA directory and
rename it to FX_SB.DRV ! (You probably will have to delete or rename the
old driver first...)

Then install the driver with SETUP.EXE and ... enjoy !

I gave this game to a lot of beta-testers before I posted it. It worked
without any Problems on ALL SOUNBLASTER CARDS and the THUNDERBOARD. 
I will try to collect all the drivers from this people and maybe post a
